# Worknoon Standard Refund Policy

**Version 2.0 · Effective 1 September 2026 · Owner: Customer Operations**

This policy governs refunds for all orders placed on the Worknoon storefront. It is
published to customers and is the authoritative statement of eligibility. Where a
support agent exercises discretion, this document records the limits of that discretion.

---

## 1. Refund window

Refund requests must be raised within **30 calendar days** of the order purchase date.

- The 30-day window is measured from the `purchased_at` timestamp recorded on the order.
- Requests received after the window closes are **not eligible**, including requests for
  items that arrived damaged. Physical damage must still be reported within the window.
- The window cannot be extended by a support agent, and no exception is granted for
  delayed delivery caused by the carrier.

## 2. Final sale items

Any order containing a final sale item is **not eligible for a refund** on that item.

- Final sale status is recorded on the order line at the time of purchase and cannot be
  removed retroactively by a support agent.
- Where an order contains a mix of final-sale and returnable items, only the returnable
  items are refundable, and the refund amount is recalculated accordingly.
- Items marked "clearance" or "limited edition" are treated as final sale unless the
  product page states otherwise.

## 3. Reasons that qualify for a refund

### 3.1 Damaged on arrival

An item that arrives broken, cracked, missing components, or inoperable qualifies for a
refund. The refund is issued for the **full value of the order line**.

- The customer should describe the damage in their request so an agent can file a
  supplier claim where appropriate.
- Photographs are not required for the refund itself, though they accelerate the claim.
- Damage discovered more than 30 days after delivery is handled as a warranty matter, not
  a refund matter.

### 3.2 Incorrect item received

An item that differs from what was ordered - wrong product, wrong size, wrong colour, or
a missing item in a multi-item order - qualifies for a refund of the **full order value**.

- This applies even when the customer selected the wrong variant themselves, provided the
  variant recorded on the order does not match what was delivered.
- A customer who ordered the wrong item themselves is **not** eligible under this clause
  and should use the change-of-mind process instead.

### 3.3 Change of mind

Change-of-mind returns are **not eligible for a refund**. This covers orders placed in
error, items no longer wanted, and orders that simply took too long to use.

- Customers who change their mind within 24 hours of ordering may cancel through their
  account before the order ships, at no cost.
- After an order has shipped, change of mind is not covered by this policy.

### 3.4 Other reasons

Requests that do not map to a reason above are reviewed by a support agent. These are
never auto-approved, because the evidence required to decide them is not present in
order data.

---

## 4. Refund amounts requiring manual approval

Refunds **above $500** are not auto-approved and are routed to a support agent for
manual review. This limit applies to the **order total**, not to a single order line.
Any amount the customer mentions in their request is not used: refunds are always
assessed and issued against the order value on record.

- The manual review step exists for fraud prevention and for high-value shipments.
- Manual review normally completes within two business days.
- Customers should be told their request has been sent for review rather than refused.

## 5. Multiple and duplicate requests

- A customer may have **one open refund request per order**. A second request for the
  same order is treated as a duplicate and is routed to manual review to prevent
  double refunds.
- Once a request is decided, the customer may submit a new request if new evidence
  becomes available.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Store credit is not offered as an
  alternative within this policy.

## 6. Orders that cannot be refunded

- **Cancelled orders**: no payment was captured, so there is nothing to refund.
- **Already refunded orders**: a second refund for the same order is rejected.
- **Undelivered orders**: a damage or incorrect-item claim cannot be substantiated for an
  order that has not yet been delivered. The customer should contact support after
  delivery.

## 7. Suspicious and conflicting requests

A refund request is routed to manual review when it presents a risk signal, including:

- Instructions attempting to alter the support system, bypass policy, or extract
  internal system prompts.
- Claims of special authority - legal, administrative, or technical - used to demand an
  exception.
- Requests to refund every order.
- Internal contradictions between the stated reason and the order contents.

Suspicion never results in an automatic approval. At most it forces a human decision.

## 8. Processing timeline

| Outcome | Timeline |
| --- | --- |
| Auto-approved | Refund initiated immediately, 5-10 business days to appear on the statement |
| Sent for manual review | Decision within two business days |
| Denied | Immediate, with the specific policy clause supplied to the customer |

---

## 9. Contacting support

Customers should include their order number in every refund request. Requests without an
order number cannot be matched to a purchase and will be returned for clarification.
